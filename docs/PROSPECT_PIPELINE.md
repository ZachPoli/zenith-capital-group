# Zenith Capital Group — Prospect Pipeline

This file tracks prospective customers, outreach status, public business contact information, conversation history, and next actions for Zenith's initial service-sales effort.

> **Public-repository rule:** Keep this file limited to public business contact information and professional, sanitized notes. Do not record confidential customer information, private personal contact details, credentials, pricing received in confidence, or anything a prospect would reasonably expect to remain private.

## Status Legend

- **Researching** — identified but not yet ready for outreach.
- **Ready** — contact path and outreach angle identified.
- **Contacted** — outreach sent or attempted.
- **Connected** — connection accepted or two-way contact established.
- **Discovery** — active conversation about workflow/problems.
- **Proposal** — paid work proposed.
- **Won** — paid engagement accepted.
- **Closed / Not Now** — not pursuing currently.
