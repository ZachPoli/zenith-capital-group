# Zenith Client Discovery Worksheet

Use this after a prospect describes a real operational problem.

## Current Workflow

- What task takes more manual work than it should?
- How is it done today?
- What tools are involved?
- Where does the information come from and where does it need to go?
- How many people touch the process?

## Frequency and Cost

- How often does it happen?
- Roughly how much time does each cycle take?
- What errors, delays, duplicate work, or re-entry occur?
- What business area is affected: inventory, production, reporting, scheduling, shipping, quoting, customer service, or management visibility?

## Desired Outcome

- What would the customer want to be different?
- What would make the improvement worth paying for?
- How could success be measured?
