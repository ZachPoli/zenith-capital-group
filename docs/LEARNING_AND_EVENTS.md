# Zenith Capital Group — Learning & Events

## Codie Sanchez: Own or Be Owned — Live Book Launch

**Date:** Friday, September 18, 2026  
**Format:** Live virtual event  
**General admission:** Free seat listed on the official event page  
**VIP option:** Paid VIP ticket is also listed on the event page

### Why it is relevant to Zenith
The event is explicitly about building a business that can operate without the owner being trapped in day-to-day execution. That aligns with Zenith's long-term objective: move from founder labor toward systems, people, recurring revenue, and ownership.

### Attend with a purpose
Do not treat the event as motivational entertainment. Prepare a short acquisition/operations question list beforehand and leave with specific actions.

Questions to answer:
- [ ] What should a first-time buyer have in place before searching for acquisitions?
- [ ] How should a small buyer think about seller financing and acquisition debt?
- [ ] What metrics best identify a business that truly runs without the owner?
- [ ] What are the biggest red flags in small software/service acquisitions?
- [ ] How should an owner decide whether to build a business from scratch or buy an existing one?
- [ ] Which systems should be installed first after acquisition?
- [ ] When is the right time to hire an operator/general manager?

### After the event
Within 24 hours:
- [ ] Write the five most useful ideas learned.
- [ ] Convert each useful idea into an action, experiment, or explicit rejection.
- [ ] Update `BUSINESS_PLAN.md` if the thesis materially changes.
- [ ] Do not buy a course, business, or financing product impulsively during the event; evaluate it later against Zenith's written plan and finances.
